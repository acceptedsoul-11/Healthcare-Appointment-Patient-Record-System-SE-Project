## Evidence handling

- Redact personal data, passwords, session cookies, authorization headers, and connection strings.
- Keep screenshots and logs under the repository's approved test-evidence location.
- Capture the full failure context without including sensitive data.
- A source-code observation is review evidence, not execution evidence.
- A CI result must identify the commit SHA and workflow/check name.
